# Difference Between AI, ML and Deep Learning

## Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI enables machines to perform tasks that require
human intelligence.

Example: ChatGPT.

## Machine Learning (ML)

ML allows computers to learn from data and make
predictions without being explicitly programmed
for every task.

Example: Email Spam Detection.

## Deep Learning (DL)

Deep Learning uses artificial neural networks
to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.

Example: Face Recognition.

## Relationship

AI is the broader concept.
ML is a subset of AI.
DL is a subset of ML.
